FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test (IMU-CET) is a national-level entrance exam conducted for admissions to undergraduate maritime courses in India, including DNS, B.Sc. Nautical Science, and B.Tech Marine Engineering.

Sponsorship is a guarantee of employment at sea upon successful completion of your academic program. Top-tier shipping companies recruit students prior to the start of their training course.

Generally, candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) at a minimum aggregate of 60%, and obtained at least 50% in English in both 10th and 12th standards. Age must be between 17 and 25 years.
